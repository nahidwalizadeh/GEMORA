Emerald

Emerald is one of the most precious gemstones in the world, admired for its rich green color and timeless beauty. It belongs to the Beryl mineral family and has been treasured for centuries by different civilizations.

Gemstone Information

  • Color: Green
  • Mineral Family: Beryl
  • Hardness: 7.5 - 8 on the Mohs scale
  • Birthstone: May
  • Origin: Afghanistan, Colombia, Zambia and Brazil

Emerald Jewelry

Emerald is widely used in elegant rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets. Its beautiful green color makes it a popular choice for luxury jewelry.

Lapis Lazuli

Lapis Lazuli is a beautiful deep-blue gemstone admired for its rich color and golden pyrite flecks. It has been treasured for thousands of years and was especially valued in ancient art, jewelry and decoration. Afghanistan is famous for its high-quality Lapis Lazuli from the Badakhshan region.

Gemstone Information

  • Color: Deep Royal Blue
  • Composition: Lazurite, Calcite and Pyrite
  • Hardness: 5 - 5.5 Mohs
  • Type: Metamorphic Rock
  • Origin: Afghanistan, Chile and Pakistan

Lapis Lazuli Jewelry

Lapis Lazuli is used in elegant rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets. Its deep blue color and golden details create a unique and timeless look in luxury jewelry.

Tourmaline

Tourmaline is a fascinating gemstone known for its remarkable variety of colors, from vibrant pink and green to deep blue and black. Some crystals even display more than one color, making each stone truly unique. It is admired for its beauty and versatility in fine jewelry.

Gemstone Information

  • Color: Pink, Green, Blue and Black
  • Mineral Family: Tourmaline Group
  • Hardness: 7 - 7.5 Mohs
  • Birthstone: October
  • Origin: Afghanistan, Brazil, Nigeria and Mozambique

Tourmaline Jewelry

Tourmaline is used in elegant rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets. Its beautiful range of colors makes it a wonderful choice for unique and luxurious jewelry designs.

Peridot

Peridot is a beautiful gemstone admired for its fresh olive-green color and natural brilliance. It belongs to the Olivine mineral group and is one of the few gemstones that occurs in only one color family. Its vibrant beauty has made it a treasured gemstone throughout history.

Gemstone Information

  • Color: Yellow-Green to Olive-Green
  • Mineral Group: Olivine
  • Hardness: 6.5 - 7 Mohs
  • Birthstone: August
  • Origin: Afghanistan, Pakistan, Myanmar and Egypt

Peridot Jewelry

Peridot is used in elegant rings, necklaces, earrings and bracelets. Its bright green color adds a fresh, graceful and luxurious touch to fine jewelry.

About GEMORA

Where Nature Meets Timeless Elegance

At GEMORA, we believe that every gemstone tells a story — a story written deep within the earth, shaped by time, and revealed through its extraordinary beauty.

GEMORA is more than just a gemstone collection. It is a celebration of nature's rarest creations, bringing together the elegance, uniqueness, and timeless charm of precious stones from around the world.

Inspired by the remarkable beauty of natural gemstones, especially the rich gemstone heritage of Afghanistan, our vision is to introduce the world to the extraordinary colors, natural formations, and exceptional character of these precious treasures.

From the deep green brilliance of emeralds to the passionate red of rubies, the timeless sparkle of diamonds, and the captivating blue of lapis lazuli, every gemstone holds a beauty that makes it truly one of a kind.

Our Story

For centuries, gemstones have fascinated people with their beauty, rarity, and mysterious origins. Formed deep within the earth over millions of years, these natural treasures represent the incredible artistry of nature.

Afghanistan is known for its remarkable gemstone heritage, offering some of the world's most fascinating natural stones. At GEMORA, we are inspired by this extraordinary heritage and the timeless relationship between gemstones and human creativity.

Our Mission

Our mission is to celebrate the natural beauty of gemstones and make their fascinating world more accessible to everyone.

Through carefully presented collections, detailed information, and elegant jewelry inspirations, we hope to create an experience that is both educational and inspiring.

Our Vision

We envision GEMORA as a place where the beauty of nature meets the elegance of fine jewelry. We believe that true luxury is about uniqueness, authenticity, craftsmanship, and the stories behind every precious creation.
